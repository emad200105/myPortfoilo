Layout-Consistency Loss: Free Geometric Supervision for Document Image Dewarping

Md Abu Hasnat, Md Atiqur Rahman Rohid, Md Sakib Uddin Niloy, Kazi Jaberul Islam Nafi, MD. Siamul Islam Apurbo, Md Juwel Mia, Muhammad Nomani Kabir

  • Proposed a training loss that uses the free region boxes of layout datasets (PubLayNet) to penalise text-line bending rather than rigid shifts; needs no OCR, line detector, or flow inversion.
  • Trained a 6.45M-parameter U-Net flow regressor (PyTorch, Kaggle GPUs) on 141,869 synthetic warps; in a controlled single-variable ablation it cut layout distortion by 6.7% at unchanged flow error and lowered Tesseract character error rate from 30.4% to 28.6%.
  • Built a reproducible DocUNet benchmark evaluation (Octave/MATLAB, pinned Tesseract) that matches published input scores to within 0.004 MS-SSIM.
